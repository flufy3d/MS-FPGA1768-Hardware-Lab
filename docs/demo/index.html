QSFP+ to DisplayPort

A concept for driving a standard DisplayPort monitor from the Arria 10 FPGA through the card's QSFP+ cage, using the four transceiver lanes, the DS250DF810 retimer in raw-data mode, and a small AUX/HPD bridge on the adapter.
