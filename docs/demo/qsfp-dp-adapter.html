Feasibility & Architecture Overview: QSFP+ to DisplayPort
  • Mechanical fit. The 18.35 × 1.0 mm body leaves 1.125 mm per side around the specified 16.1 mm DP shell. The mating tongue narrows to 16.42 mm. Confirm shell tabs, retention, wipe and tolerances against selected connector drawings.
  • Retimer bypass. U17 is a 135-ball, 13 × 8 mm DS250DF810. The proposed I2C Bus#1 setup uses CDR bypass for DP rates of 1.62–8.1 Gbps. OVRD_OUT_MUX=1 and CDR_RESET=1 are proposed settings. Verify the mux selection, jitter and link training at each rate.
  • Sideband & AUX channel. The self-contained bridge uses zero flying wires. QSFP+ powers a CH32V003F4U6 I2C slave and an SN65MLVD201. The MCU is intended to encode and decode 1 Mbps Manchester-II AUX frames, control turnaround and return HPD events via open-drain IntL. The transceiver drives AC-coupled AUX+/− with 100 kΩ connector-side bias. Host commands, bridge firmware, termination and DP electrical compliance require validation.
  • AC coupling. The model assumes host-side series capacitors. Four equal-length differential routes run directly to DP with no adapter capacitors. The 100 Ω target requires stack-up and signal-integrity verification.

Full-wrap sleeve · Assembly and sourcing
  • DP sourcing candidate. HDGC DP-GD-119PWB, LCSC / JLCPCB part C7425332. Use LCSC for individual parts and JLCPCB Standard PCBA for assembly. The model illustrates a mid-mount socket. Confirm the recommended PCB thickness, cutout, mounting tabs, shell envelope and signal rating for this exact part before releasing the PCB or sleeve.
  • Fit. The sleeve follows the DP shell chamfer. The front face and socket project 5 mm beyond the cage. Rounded ribs on all four sides give about 0.1 mm of nominal compression per side in TPU 95A; PETG needs extra clearance.
  • Assembly. Feed the socket into the sleeve from the rear, guide the PCB edges into the slots and engage the two clips. To remove, disconnect the cable, pull the front frame straight out of the cage, spread the clips and slide the sleeve off the PCB.
  • Trial print. Print in PETG (recommended) or TPU 95A with the front face on the bed. PETG needs 0.1–0.2 mm clearance because the ribs do not compress. The STL uses millimetres. Check clip supports in the slicer and dry-fit in an unpowered cage.
